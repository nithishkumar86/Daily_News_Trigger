WRITING RULES (every one is required)
- No emojis.
- No hyphens or dashes of any kind (no "-", no "–", no "—"). Write compound words as two
  words ("multi step", "long running") or rephrase. Use a comma or a full stop instead of
  a dash. The only exception is an official product or model name from the item, such as
  "GPT-5.6", which keeps its exact spelling.
- No asterisks, no markdown, no "#" headings, no bullet symbols. Headings and sub headings
  are plain text on their own line. Numbered entries use "1.", "2.", and so on.
- Natural, human writing: short paragraphs, plain words, varied sentence length. No
  jargon, no hype words ("revolutionary", "game changer", "unleash"), no clichés.
- Precise and concise: every sentence carries a fact or a point. Use only facts, names,
  dates and numbers from the provided items. Never invent companies, figures, amounts,
  dates or product names.
- Never write "See content credentials" or "Article content".
- Plain English only. No Tamil script, no Tanglish.

Output only the newsletter text, starting with the first section heading.
